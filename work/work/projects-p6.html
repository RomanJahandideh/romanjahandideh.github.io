SocialEyes

SocialEyes investigates how adaptive virtual agents can support structured social-skills learning in a safe and responsive environment. Developed as a research prototype, the project explores communication practice for children with autism spectrum disorder, where social learning often benefits from repetition, predictability, and carefully managed feedback. By creating a virtual classroom with AI-driven agents that respond to gaze and behavioral patterns, the system offers a setting in which social cues can be practiced with less pressure than in real-world rehearsal.

The methodology combines human-centered interaction design with probabilistic decision-making. The system uses a Bayesian model to generate agent responses based on user attention and social context, while distinct agent personalities create variability in tone, emotion, and response behavior. This creates a social environment that feels dynamic without becoming unpredictable. In addition, the project uses validated facial expressions to create a more legible emotional language for the virtual agents, helping users interpret and respond to social signals more clearly.

The project’s value lies not only in the interaction model but also in its educational framing. SocialEyes treats social learning as a structured practice that requires both repetition and reflection. By allowing users to rehearse engagement strategies in a controlled immersive environment, the system creates an opportunity for gradual confidence building and iterative learning. The design is intentionally supportive: the experience is shaped to reduce anxiety while preserving the complexity of social interaction.

Project outcomes suggest strong potential for immersive technologies in supportive education and therapeutic design. While the current prototype remains a proof of concept and does not establish clinical effectiveness, it demonstrates how VR and AI can work together to create emotionally informed training experiences. SocialEyes stands as an example of how digital systems can extend social learning beyond instruction into a design space grounded in empathy, agency, and careful responsiveness.

Tech: Virtual reality, adaptive AI agents, Bayesian decision models, eye-tracking, social-skills training design.

Role: Research, interaction design, XR prototyping, adaptive systems design.

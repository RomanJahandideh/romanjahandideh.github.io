SocialEyes

SocialEyes is a virtual reality social-skills training system designed to help users practice communication in a supportive, adaptive, and low-pressure environment. The project addresses a recurring challenge in social learning: people often struggle to rehearse difficult or unfamiliar communication patterns in real life without feeling vulnerable, overstimulated, or uncertain. By creating AI-driven characters with distinct personalities and responsive behaviors, the project offers a controlled setting in which users can safely explore conversational scenarios, interpret social cues, and build confidence gradually.

The methodology integrates user-centered design, immersive prototyping, and adaptive interaction logic. Rather than presenting a static script, the system responds to user behavior in real time, encouraging naturalistic conversation and making the social context feel dynamic. Character responses are designed to reflect different communication styles, emotional states, and interpersonal challenges, creating a more realistic range of social situations. This makes the experience valuable not only as a training tool but also as a design research project on how artificial agents can support learning and emotional safety.

The project is especially relevant for inclusive and neurodiverse learning contexts, where conventional social training may be difficult to access or emotionally taxing. Through structured interactions and repeatable scenarios, users can practice communication strategies in a setting that allows reflection, revision, and gradual improvement. The system does not remove the complexity of real social experience, but it creates a more manageable environment for learning through repetition and adaptive feedback.

Project outcomes highlight the broader potential of immersive systems for educational and therapeutic design. SocialEyes demonstrates how VR, AI, and interaction design can work together to create supportive learning experiences that are both emotionally intelligent and highly practical. It stands as a strong example of how digital environments can be designed to empower people as they develop communication confidence and social fluency.

Tech: Virtual reality, AI-driven character behavior, adaptive interaction design, social skills training systems.

Role: Research, Interaction Design, XR Prototyping, Human-Centered Experience Design.

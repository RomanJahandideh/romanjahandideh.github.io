SocialEyes

SocialEyes is an adaptive virtual reality social-skills training system designed to support communication learning in situations where real-world practice can feel intimidating, unpredictable, or inaccessible. The project uses AI-driven characters with distinct personalities and behavioral patterns to create a responsive social environment where users can rehearse conversations, interpret social cues, and learn how to manage repeated interaction scenarios in a controlled setting.

The system moves beyond static training scripts by building a dynamic environment in which characters respond to the user’s behavior, pace, and communication choices. This allows the experience to feel more lifelike and encourages users to practice practical social skills such as eye contact, turn-taking, emotional recognition, and conversational timing. In this way, the virtual space becomes a rehearsal studio for interpersonal learning rather than just a demonstration tool.

At the intersection of VR, AI interaction design, and inclusive learning, SocialEyes explores how immersive systems can support people with neurodiverse communication needs. The project demonstrates the potential of adaptive, human-centered interfaces to create safer and more engaging environments for learning communication in a way that feels supportive, measurable, and personalized.

Tech: Virtual reality, AI-driven character behavior, adaptive interaction design, social skills training systems.

Role: Research, Interaction Design, XR Prototyping, Human-Centered Experience Design.

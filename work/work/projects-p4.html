Creating Immersive Virtual Landscapes

This project examines how virtual environments can communicate depth, scale, and spatial clarity through visual design rather than relying on realism alone. In head-mounted displays, users are highly sensitive to subtle issues in contrast, color, transparency, and perspective. By designing immersive landscapes with these perceptual factors in mind, the project investigates how digital environments can feel more believable, readable, and comfortable.

The work focuses on the visual language of landscape design inside VR, exploring how natural forms, atmospheric effects, and layered depth cues influence the user’s sense of orientation. Rather than treating the environment as a passive backdrop, the project treats landscape as a dynamic interface for spatial understanding, guiding attention and supporting navigation through composition, value contrast, and environmental rhythm.

Developed through iterative design and user evaluation, the project bridges environment design, perceptual research, and immersive storytelling. It highlights the importance of visual hierarchy in virtual space and demonstrates how design decisions can influence not only aesthetic quality but also usability, comfort, and presence.

Tech: Unreal Engine, immersive environment design, perceptual testing, virtual scene construction.

Role: Research, Environment Design, VR Experience Development, User-Centered Design.

About

Introduction

Researcher working across architecture, landscape design, and computational systems: human-AI co-design, performance-informed design, and embodied interaction. Holds a Master's in Landscape Architecture Design (Istanbul Technical University), with VR research on depth perception and environmental legibility in head-mounted displays, published at eCAADe and submitted to CAADRIA. Architectural project work spans generative, algorithm-driven form and performance-integrated design, including passive-cooling facades and stormwater and carbon-management strategies. Current work at Simon Fraser University builds interactive systems that respond to physiological signals in real time, methodology now extending into a paper in preparation on AI in architectural design.

Research projects and publications

Research in physiological interaction, virtual environments, and computational design. Select a project to read its methods, findings, and current status.

Embedding Physiological Environments

A bioadaptive Unity environment that maps heart rate and HRV to environmental feedback, evaluated in a within-subjects study with 24 participants.

Reading a Remote Teammate's Body

An investigation of ambient physiological cues for awareness during synchronous remote collaboration. The paper is in revision for an ACM resubmission in 2027.

Optimizing Eye-Tracking Locomotion in Virtual Environments

A study of gaze-guided locomotion, comfort, and spatial understanding in virtual architectural and landscape environments. Submitted to CAADRIA 2024.

Creating Immersive Virtual Landscapes

Research on depth perception and environmental legibility in head-mounted virtual landscapes, published in the proceedings of eCAADe 2023.

Testing Cybersickness in Passive and Active VR Locomotion

A comparative study of passive and active VR locomotion using the iSpace Lab benchmark to examine cybersickness and mitigation strategies.

SocialEyes: Adaptive VR Social Skills Training for Autism

A course research prototype using Bayesian virtual agents for structured social skills practice, with computational evaluation of agent response probabilities.

A Portfolio You Walk Through: Gamifying Access to Information

A browser-based portfolio that uses twelve architectural levels and spatial interaction to explore progressive disclosure of personal and professional information.

Parametric Massing Generator

A traceable zoning-to-form system that extracts eight constraints, cites their source, independently reviews the result, and generates massing from simple lots to urban blocks.

Zoning Literacy Assistant

A grounded zoning assistant that retrieves and cites bylaw clauses, verifies its own answers, declines unsupported questions, and computes site feasibility deterministically.

Vancouver View Corridor Massing

A verified Rhino and Grasshopper study showing how precise floor-by-floor trimming recovers 39.5% more area than a conservative flat-cap method beneath Vancouver's view corridors.

Environmental Massing Agents

A study of specialized AI agents and deterministic environmental analysis, evaluated through four hypotheses concerning optimization and geometric design tradeoffs.

Vancouver Climate Futures

A browser-based serious game connecting adaptation decisions with a selectable 2050s/2100s climate projection forecast, cited Vancouver sea-level projections, and illustrative heat and energy indices.
About

Digital 3D environment researcher building generative, AI-assisted design systems for the built environment, from computational form to physiologically adaptive interfaces.

The question I'm chasing: how can digital environments be designed to optimize the real, physical spaces we live in and the lives we live inside them?

Research Interests

Generative & AI-assisted design for the built environment · Physiologically adaptive & biofeedback-driven systems · Embodied interaction in virtual & game environments · Human-centered empirical user studies

Researcher building digital 3D environments and generative, AI-assisted design systems, with a background in architecture, landscape design, and VR research into how space is experienced. That background, built during a Master's at Istanbul Technical University, first raised the question above.

That question now drives research at Simon Fraser University's iViz Lab, combining generative AI-assisted design with physiologically adaptive, biofeedback-driven interaction. Also teaches graphic design and 3D animation at SFU.
